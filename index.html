ABOUT KANHA UPVAN VALLEY

Plots for the life
you imagine,
planned for tomorrow.

Shri Kanha Upvan Valley offers residential, commercial, farm house and duplex plots in Balu, Ranchi. The project is positioned near Devi Mandap and Pithoria Chowk, with connectivity towards the Ring Road and nearby city landmarks.

The project plan highlights internal roads along with essential community infrastructure, creating a plotted development designed around access, security and everyday convenience.
